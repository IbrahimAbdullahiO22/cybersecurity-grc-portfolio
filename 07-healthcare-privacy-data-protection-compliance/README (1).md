# Project 07 — Healthcare Privacy & Data Protection Compliance

**Portfolio case study | PrairieTrust Healthcare Services (fictional)**

## Overview
Integrated preliminary assessment of the fictional **PatientConnect** cloud platform, involving PHI processing for 12,500 patients and eight supplied observations (CAP-01–CAP-08). Focus: HIPAA/HITECH, NIST Privacy Framework, ISO/IEC 27701:2025, and conditional GDPR / CCPA/CPRA applicability.

## Contents
- `PatientConnect_Privacy_Assessment_Workbook.xlsx` — observation register, crosswalk, unexecuted testing workpapers and preliminary risk ratings.
- `Executive_Privacy_Assessment_Report.docx` — management-facing preliminary assessment.
- `Independent_Testing_Workpapers.docx` — proposed audit procedures for CAP-02, CAP-04 and CAP-07.
- `Management_Remediation_Roadmap.docx` — all eight observations, owners, proposed timelines and closure criteria.

## Key conclusions
- CAP-02: downstream subcontractor assurances not supplied; absence of required agreement **not established**.
- CAP-03: sensitive appointment reminder complaint; impermissible disclosure and reportable breach **not established**.
- CAP-04: 14 privileged vendor accounts; authorization and review evidence **not supplied**.
- CAP-08: analytics module excluded from SOC 2 scope; exclusion **does not prove insecurity**.
- All CAP items remain open for evidence collection and validation. No tests were executed against a real system.

## Methodology and limitations
Mapped preliminary observations to potentially relevant obligations; differentiated design/documentation gaps from operating failures; prioritized risks using an illustrative qualitative matrix; designed population-completeness, inspection and reperformance procedures; proposed risk-based remediation and formal closure criteria. Regulatory applicability requires separate assessment. **This project is entirely synthetic, is not a production audit, does not claim certification or regulatory compliance, and contains no real patient information.**
