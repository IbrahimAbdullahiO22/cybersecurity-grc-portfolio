# Project 06 — Audit Findings, Remediation & Continuous Monitoring

> **Portfolio simulation.** PrairieTrust Healthcare Services (PTHS) is fictional. All change records, evidence, and management updates are training scenarios. No production audit, live control testing, or independent retesting was performed.

## Engagement objective
Assess change-management approval observations, evaluate management-reported corrective actions, design independent retesting, and communicate evidence-based closure recommendations.

## Scope and approach
- **System:** fictional cloud financial management application.
- **Audit period:** July–September 2026.
- **Illustrative population:** 50 reported changes; five initial sample records. No statistical population inference is claimed.
- **Criteria:** simulated PTHS approval policy and emergency-change procedure; NIST SP 800-53 CM-3 as reference guidance.
- **Method:** examine records, interview process owners, propose authorized tests, track remediation and residual risk.

## Findings and status
| Finding | Observation / update | Current status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F-01 | CHG-002 approval recorded after implementation; management provided workflow screenshot and approved ticket | Pending independent validation |
| F-02 | CHG-004 approval not located; management provided revised emergency procedure | Pending independent validation |
| F-03 | Monthly exception reporting started; one report supplied | Pending independent validation |

F-01 and F-02 are preliminary approval observations. Emergency classification and alternative approval records must be checked before confirming policy violations. F-03 is a separate monitoring weakness identified for follow-up. None of the three is closed.

## Files
- `PTHS_Audit_Findings_POAM_and_Retesting.xlsx` — findings, corrective actions, retesting plan, evidence register.
- `PTHS_Audit_Findings_and_Retesting_Report.docx` — scope, observations, planned test steps, and closure limitations.
- `PTHS_Management_Follow_Up_Brief.docx` — concise management update and decisions requested.

## Quality and evidence boundaries
No audit test results or successful remediation are fabricated. Evidence marked as management-provided is not independent assurance. Root causes, owners, due dates and risk ratings remain unconfirmed unless explicitly noted as proposed. Closure requires objective retesting and authorized sign-off. Historical exceptions remain in the record even after process improvements.
