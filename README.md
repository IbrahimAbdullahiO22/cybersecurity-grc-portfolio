Welcome to my Cybersecurity Governance, Risk & Compliance portfolio.

### Project 01 — Information Security Governance

**Focus:** Governance structures, information security policies, control ownership, policy exceptions, and governance findings.

**Deliverables:** Information Security Policy, simulated governance findings, supporting documentation.

**Frameworks:** NIST CSF 2.0, ISO 27001, COBIT, CIS Controls.

### Project 02 — Enterprise Risk Assessment

**Focus:** Risk identification, likelihood and impact assessment, inherent and residual risk, risk treatment, risk ownership, and executive reporting.

**Deliverables:** Enterprise risk register, risk assessment documentation, risk treatment recommendations, executive risk brief.

**Frameworks:** NIST SP 800-30, ISO 31000, FAIR, NIST CSF.

### Project 03 — NIST CSF 2.0 Assessment

**Focus:** Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover, Current and Target Profiles, evidence assessment, gap analysis, and remediation prioritization.

**Planned deliverables:** CSF assessment, control gap register, remediation roadmap, management report.

**Frameworks:** NIST CSF 2.0, NIST SP 800-53, ISO 27001, CIS Controls.

### Project 04 — NIST RMF & Security Control Assessment

**Focus:** System categorization, security control selection, assessment procedures, System Security Plans, POA&M, authorization decisions, and continuous monitoring.

**Frameworks:** NIST SP 800-37, 800-53, 800-53A, 800-171, FIPS 199, CMMC.

### Project 05 — ISO 27001, COBIT, CIS Controls & IT Audit

**Focus:** ISMS implementation, Statement of Applicability, audit planning, IT general controls, evidence evaluation, sampling, and control effectiveness testing.

**Frameworks:** ISO 27001/27002, COBIT, CIS Controls, applicable IT audit criteria.

### Project 06 — Audit Findings & Remediation

**Focus:** Audit findings, root cause analysis, risk-based severity, corrective action plans, POA&M, retesting, closure validation, and continuous control monitoring.

### Project 07 — Healthcare, Privacy & Data Protection

**Focus:** Healthcare security, ePHI, privacy governance, data protection, privacy impact assessments, and compliance gap analysis.

**Frameworks and regulations:** HIPAA, HITECH, GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, NIST Privacy Framework, ISO 27701.

### Project 08 — Assurance, Financial Controls & Third-Party Risk

**Focus:** SOC reporting, SOX ITGC, payment security, financial-sector safeguards, vendor due diligence, contract security, and continuous third-party monitoring.

**Frameworks and regulations:** SOC 1, SOC 2, SSAE 18, SOX, COSO, PCI DSS, GLBA, FFIEC, SIG, NIST SP 800-161.

### Project 09 — IAM, Cloud Security & Business Continuity

**Focus:** Identity lifecycle, privileged access, access reviews, MFA, cloud security controls, business impact analysis, disaster recovery, and resilience.

**Frameworks:** NIST SP 800-53, CSA CCM, ISO 27017, ISO 27018, ISO 22301, NIST SP 800-34.

### Project 10 — Enterprise GRC Platforms & Automation

**Focus:** Enterprise risk workflows, privacy and vendor assessments, audit management, GRC dashboards, reporting automation, and data quality.

**Target platforms:** Archer, OneTrust, ServiceNow IRM, CISO Assistant, Eramba, AuditBoard, Vanta, Drata, Excel, Power BI, Python.

### Project 11 — Multi-Industry Enterprise GRC Simulation

**Industries:** Healthcare, banking, insurance, technology, manufacturing, energy, government contracting, and retail.

**Specialized frameworks:** IEC 62443, NIST SP 800-82, CMMC, financial-sector guidance, and applicable sector-specific requirements.

### Project 12 — Integrated Enterprise GRC Capstone

**Focus:** End-to-end enterprise risk and compliance engagement, including governance, scoping, framework selection, risk assessment, control testing, findings, remediation, third-party risk, reporting, and management presentation.

The final project is designed to demonstrate independently applied GRC knowledge and professional communication.

## Practical Learning Methodology

Projects use a structured approach:

1. Learn the relevant framework and business requirements.
2. Analyze a realistic enterprise security scenario.
3. Simulate professional GRC software workflows.
4. Perform risk assessments, control mapping, and evidence evaluation.
5. Document findings and recommend corrective actions.
6. Complete authorized hands-on application laboratories where available.
7. Prepare professional reports and supporting artifacts.
8. Assess practical proficiency and document demonstrated capabilities.
