# Cybersecurity Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) Portfolio

## About This Portfolio

This repository documents my hands-on cybersecurity Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) professional development through simulated enterprise assessments, security governance documentation, risk management exercises, and evidence-based reporting.

The projects demonstrate my ability to apply cybersecurity governance and risk management concepts to realistic business scenarios and communicate findings in a structured, professional manner.

**Focus Areas:** Cybersecurity Governance | Enterprise Risk Management | IT Audit & Controls | Security Compliance | Risk Treatment | Evidence Validation

### Project 02 — Enterprise Cybersecurity Risk Assessment & Risk Register

**Organization:** PrairieTrust Healthcare Services (fictional training scenario)  
**Status:** Completed simulated assessment; remediation recommendations are not represented as implemented

Conducted a simulated enterprise cybersecurity risk assessment addressing access governance, former-employee account deprovisioning, sensitive information access, and vulnerability management.

- Identified and documented four cybersecurity risk scenarios.
- Evaluated likelihood and business impact using an illustrative 5×5 risk scoring methodology.
- Prepared risk register entries, proposed risk ownership, and mitigation recommendations.

- Prepared a management-facing cybersecurity risk summary.

**Deliverables:**
- Sanitized Enterprise Cybersecurity Risk Register
- Enterprise Risk Committee Management Brief
- Project Documentation

## Project Methodology

Portfolio projects follow a structured approach:

1. Define the simulated business context and assessment scope.
2. Identify governance requirements, cybersecurity risks, or control expectations.
3. Analyze available evidence and document findings.
4. Propose risk-based corrective actions and ownership.
5. Establish verification requirements and management reporting.
6. Prepare sanitized documentation suitable for professional review.

## Scope and Professional Disclaimer

All PrairieTrust Healthcare Services scenarios are fictional and created for independent professional development. These projects do not represent employment at the simulated organization, actual patient data, confirmed security incidents, implemented production controls, real organizational approvals, or official audit conclusions.

Risk ratings, findings, remediation plans, and assessment decisions are based on training scenarios and their stated assumptions. Simulated authorization or closure decisions must not be interpreted as real-world organizational approvals.
