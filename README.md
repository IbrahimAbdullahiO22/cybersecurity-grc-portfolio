# Cybersecurity GRC Portfolio — Information Security Governance

## Project 01: Information Security Policy Development

**Organization:** PrairieTrust Healthcare Services (fictional training scenario)  
**Document:** PTHS-ISP-001 | Version 0.1 | **Draft — Not Approved**

### Project overview
Developed and reviewed a simulated Information Security Policy for a fictional healthcare organization. The exercise focused on creating clear governance requirements, identifying policy inconsistencies, documenting corrective actions, and verifying the consolidated draft.

### Project deliverables
- [Information Security Policy (PDF)](PTHS-ISP-001-v0.1-Draft.pdf)
- [Editable policy draft (Word)](PTHS-ISP-001-v0.1-Draft.docx)
- [Simulated quality-review finding register](simulated-finding-register.md)

### GRC topics covered
- Information security governance, policy scope, and responsibilities
- Joiner–Mover–Leaver access lifecycle and least privilege
- Multifactor authentication and formal security exceptions
- Information protection, endpoint security, and incident reporting
- Compliance monitoring, evidence validation, and finding closure
- Annual and event-driven policy review, version control, and approval integrity

### Simulated quality-review process
Four document-quality findings (QF-01 through QF-04) were identified and addressed in a training review. The finding register records the issues, proposed corrections, and simulated review status. No real technical controls were tested.

### Skills demonstrated
Policy drafting; governance analysis; requirements writing; quality assurance; simulated finding management; document control; evidence-based reasoning.

### Scope and limitations
**This is a self-directed training project, not employment experience or a real organizational policy.** PrairieTrust Healthcare Services is fictional. The policy is an unapproved draft and has not been implemented, independently audited, or legally validated. The findings were closed only within the simulated document review.

### Interview summary
“I developed a simulated healthcare information security policy, corrected governance inconsistencies through a structured review, and documented the findings and revisions. I kept the policy clearly labeled as a draft for a fictional organization.”
