# Preliminary Executive Technology-Risk Brief

**To:** Chief Information Security Officer  
**From:** Enterprise GRC & Technology Risk Analyst (simulated role)  
**Organization:** PrairieTrust Healthcare Services (fictional)  
**Reporting deadline:** Five business days

## Executive summary
The preliminary assessment identifies significant concerns involving EHR access governance, overdue critical vulnerability remediation, vendor onboarding and closure verification. Twelve former-employee EHR accounts remain active and a critical scheduling-server vulnerability is 14 calendar days overdue. A separate High IAM issue is seven days overdue; an IRM issue is Closed without verification and evidence. No supplied evidence establishes an actual incident, exploitation, unauthorized access or patient-data compromise.

## Immediate management priorities
1. **EHR access:** IAM Manager urgently investigates and resolves inappropriate former-employee access; validates removal and reviews activity; supplies privileged-review and MFA evidence.
2. **Critical vulnerability:** Infrastructure Manager expedites remediation, substantiates any compensating controls and obtains independent technical validation.
3. **Issue governance:** GRC Manager investigates unsupported Closed status, escalates overdue High IAM issue and documents owners, dates and verification.
4. **MediBridge:** Maintain production onboarding hold pending PHI/business-associate assessment, BAA if required, SOC 2 exception review, safeguard and subprocessor validation, and approvals.

## Ownership
Business owners remain accountable for risks; IAM and Infrastructure Managers implement corrections; TPRM coordinates vendor assurance; Privacy/Legal evaluate HIPAA and contracts; GRC maintains the risk register and reporting; Internal Audit independently tests as appropriate.

## Five-business-day deliverables
Preliminary risk register, prioritized remediation plan, outstanding evidence requests, vendor recommendation, supported dashboard, and open decisions/limitations.

## Assurance limitations and recommendation
Missing evidence is not proof of a control failure, and the limited packet cannot support enterprise-wide closure metrics. No new risk acceptance has been approved. Prioritize confirmed access and vulnerability conditions, investigate unsupported closures, and require authorized, evidence-based decisions before declaring issues resolved.
